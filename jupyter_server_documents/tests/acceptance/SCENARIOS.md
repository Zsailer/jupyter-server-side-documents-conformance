## Decisions taken as a best effort

Enforced like any other, and open to change: edit `reference`, `floor`, `expect` and `policy.decision` of the scenario in `suite.yaml`. The rule of thumb they follow is in the comment at the top of that file.

- **P1**: The file wins. A restore from a backup, or a sync that put an older copy on disk, has to hold even against a tab that still has newer content, or nothing could ever be restored. The newer content is the price.
- **Z1**: The typing is never discarded because its cell moved at the same moment: the move and the typing both survive. Whoever moves a cell does not mean to throw away what somebody else is typing in it.
- **Z2**: A cell exists once. After two concurrent moves of the same cell the later copy is removed.
- **Z3**: The deletion wins, as it does between two people who are online: the cell stays deleted and the typing goes with it. Whoever deleted the cell may have meant its content to go too, and bringing it back as a recovered copy would show it again to everyone, the person who deleted it included.
- **Z4**: The merge decides, as it does between two people who are online: both rewrites are kept, one after the other, and nothing either person typed is lost. Choosing a winner, or splitting the second rewrite into a cell of its own, would treat an offline return differently from online editing; that is a change of product, not a repair.
- **Z5**: An outside change is merged with the unsaved typing, not applied over it: both survive. The file stays the source of truth for what it carries, but it does not erase what it has not seen.
- **Z6**: The bound is accepted. A server that dies loses what it had not saved, if no tab still holds it: at most an autosave interval of typing, and nothing else. The notebook is the last saved state, whole. Closing that window would cost a disk write per update; revisit if that cost becomes acceptable.
- **Z7**: The typing is kept: merged into its cell when it can be matched, as a cell marked "recovered" when it cannot.

## Waiting for a decision

- **AI5**: An agent rewrites a cell while a person is typing in it. Who gives way: are both kept (as between two people who are online), does the agent wait until the cell is quiet, or does the person decide on the agent's rewrite?
- **AI6**: A deletion wins over typing made in the same cell at the same moment (Z3). Does the answer change when the one who deleted the cell is an agent and not a person?
- **AI7**: An edit that a person types is not saved before it is relayed (Z6). Should an edit made by an agent be saved before the agent is told that it worked?
