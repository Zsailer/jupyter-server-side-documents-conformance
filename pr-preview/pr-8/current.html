    Control: a tab reconnects to a notebook the server rebuilt

    The everyday case. A tab loses its connection, the server lets go of the notebook and later builds it again from the file, and the tab reconnects. Everything the tab says reaches the server. Many of the stories below vary this one.

    1. Your tab opens the notebook
    2. Your tab loses its connection (laptop lid closed)
    3. Server lets go of the notebook, then builds it again from the file
    4. Your tab reconnects and sends its answer; the answer arrives
    5. Your tab the connection drops once more
    6. Your tab reconnects again
    What the product doesNothing is lost or duplicated: the tab, the server and the file hold the same three cells.
